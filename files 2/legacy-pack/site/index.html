// The Man Behind The Archive
About Jack
Jack Minnier is a Vietnam veteran, American Legion post member, and the self-appointed keeper of the Minnier family record. He has spent years building this archive by hand — paper, photographs, Ancestry.com records, and the kind of memory that only comes from genuinely caring about where you came from.
He is building this for the children. For their children. For everyone who will carry the name Minnier — or its branches — into a future he may not see. That is not a small thing. That is everything.
🇺🇸
Every year, Jack visits every cemetery in rural Pennsylvania with a bundle of American flags. He hikes the hills — with all kinds of old man problems — and replaces every tattered flag with a fresh one. Alone. To honor his fellow soldiers. He does not do this for recognition. He does it because someone has to, and he is the someone.
